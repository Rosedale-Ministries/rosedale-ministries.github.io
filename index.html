FAQ

Questions people ask.

Do I have to commit forever?

No. Every new volunteer starts with a six month season to try, weekly, every other week, or monthly, with a check in at six months.

What if I already know where I want to serve?

Great. The gifts test isn't there to talk you out of it. It affirms where God has already been stirring you.

How will I know when I'm scheduled?

Your team lead schedules you through your team's scheduling tool, and you'll get a reminder before you serve. See what that looks like.

Can I serve at Point Church or in the Spanish ministry?

Yes. The same process works across every Rosedale ministry. Choose Outreach on the form and mention Point Church or the Spanish ministry, and we'll connect you with that team.

Who do I talk to with questions?

Call or text 443.371.8470 anytime and a pastor will follow up with you.

Step by step

Here's how it works.

  1. 1

    You fill out the interest form

    Pick the teams that interest you. It takes about two minutes.

  2. 2

    A welcome text and email

    Soon after you sign up, a pastor reaches out personally with your next step.

  3. 3

    Take the spiritual gifts test

    Your results help us place you where God has wired you to serve.

  4. 4

    Your team lead reaches out

    They'll get to know you and set up a time for you to shadow.

  5. 5

    Your first serving request

    You'll get a text and an email from Church Community Builder asking you to serve on a date. Tap the link and choose Accept.

  6. 6

    Manage your schedule

    In the CCB app, My Serving shows every date you're scheduled. Going out of town? Add a blockout so your lead knows.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Who we are

This site is run by Rosedale Baptist Church, 9202 Philadelphia Road, Baltimore, MD 21237. It helps people join a serve team and helps our team leads care for their volunteers.

What we collect

When you fill out the Serve Team Interest form we collect your name, email, mobile number, mobile carrier, the ministries you are interested in, and anything else you choose to tell us. If you take the spiritual gifts test we also keep your answers and results. Team leads who sign in give us their email address so we can send them a sign in code.

Why we collect it

We use your information only to welcome you, follow up with you about serving, place you on a team, schedule you, and send you reminders. We may contact you by email, phone call, or text message about serving at Rosedale.

Text messages

By giving us your mobile number you agree to receive text messages from Rosedale about serving. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Mobile numbers and text messaging consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

What we never do

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with the Rosedale pastors and team leads who serve with you.

Where it is kept

Your information is stored in Church Community Builder, our church management system, and in our Google Workspace account. Videos play directly on this site and do not track you. Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information, or stop contacting you, at any time. Call or text 443.371.8470 and a pastor will take care of it.

Children

This site is meant for adults and students who are signing up to serve. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
